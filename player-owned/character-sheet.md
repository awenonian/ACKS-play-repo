## Class Powers

- **Diplomacy** — +1 on all reaction rolls when parleying with intelligent creatures.
- **Bribery** — +1 reaction for a day's pay, +2 for a week's, +3 for a month's. Deniable; you are only
  caught out on an **unmodified 2 on 2d6**.
- **Expert Bargaining** — buy at **−10%**, sell at **+10%**. Against another bargainer, opposed reaction
  rolls decide who gets the discount.
- **Expert Traveling** — Driving (taken).
- **Mercantile Network** — in any market you've entered before, treat it as **one class larger**, or take
  **+1 market impact**, whichever helps. (Not for passive investments.)
- **Multilingual** — three bonus languages (taken; see below).
- **Pathfinding** — **+4** to avoid getting lost in familiar territory. At 1st level that's the settled
  Borderlands plus one 24-mile hex beyond settlement. Radius grows one hex per level.
- **Treachery** — if you reach a **Friendly** reaction via Diplomacy or Bribery, you may attack
  treacherously: everyone within 30′ rolls surprise at **−3**. Doesn't work if their negotiator is
  also a venturer.

**Henchmen: up to 5**, at **+1 loyalty** (4 + CHA modifier).
