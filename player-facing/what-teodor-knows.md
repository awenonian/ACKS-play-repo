# What Teodor Knows

*Player-facing. Things Teodor could tell you over a drink. Uncertainty is allowed here —
some of this is what people say, not what is true.*

## The World, Briefly

Four years ago the Tarkaun took the legions west to fight the Skysos. Last year word came
back that they'd been surrounded and destroyed. Nobody has confirmed it and nobody has
disproved it. The Borderlands has been drawing its own conclusions.

The border forts are still there. Most of them are not really manned. Two on the eastern
line have fallen in the last eighteen months — Hasp, and the little one at Wier whose name
Teodor never learned. Beastmen come across from the Waste when they feel like it.

The great families of the Empire are reportedly at each other's throats instead of anyone's
defense. Out here that translates to: no relief, no pay chests, no one coming.

## The Borderlands

The strip between the Meniri Mountains, the ocean, and two rivers — the Krysivor and the
Mirmen. Contested for three thousand years, and the landscape shows it. There are more
ruins out here than there are villages. Most of them are worthless. Some are not.

Power is decentralizing to whoever can pay soldiers. That means landowners, toll-holders,
and a few prefects who've stopped waiting for orders.

## Tellys Ford

A crossing-town on the Krysivor. Perhaps four hundred souls, more on market-day. A stone
bridge the Aurans built and a ford beside it for anyone who doesn't want to pay.

- **Lady Dessa Korvane** holds the toll rights by an imperial grant her great-grandfather
  bought. Widowed, mid-forties. She has been hiring men lately, which everyone has noticed
  and nobody has said anything about.
- **Vitte Sarn**, factor. Holds Teodor's note. Not cruel, but not sentimental either.
- **Father Anselm Vurro**, priest of Calefa, keeps the burning-ground. Fussy about rite.
  Teodor has known him since he was small.

## Kalyx Gate

The border fort eleven miles south. **The one that's holding.** Prefect Naevio Ostren
commands what's left of the Third Cohort of the XII Legion, and by every account he's the
one honest officer left on this stretch of border. Refugees go there. The roads within a
day's ride of it are the safest in the province — the beastmen don't come near.

People speak well of Ostren. Teodor has never met him.

## The Problem

Last autumn Kalyx Gate placed a standing order through the Tellys Ford factors: grey wool
broadcloth, in bulk, before midsummer. Grey is mourning cloth. Forts always need it. It was
the safest order anyone could ask for.

Teodor borrowed **120 gold suns from Vitte Sarn** and bought thirty bolts.

In the spring the order was cancelled. The note said, in full: **"No further need."**

Everyone assumed the fort was economizing. Teodor now owns thirty bolts of a cloth that
has precisely one use, in a province of four thousand people, and Sarn wants his money on
market-day.

Twelve days.
