# Logistics — the numbers Teodor works with

*Player-facing. Rules figures, verified against the rulebook, plus Judge's rulings where the
book is silent (marked as such).*

## Weight

- **1 stone ≈ 10 lbs.** It's an abstract measure of weight *and* bulk.
- **1,000 coins = 1 stone.** (So 520 gold suns is half a stone. It isn't heavy. It's *loud*.)
- **1 stone of food = one week's eating for one man-sized creature.**
- Armor = 1 stone per point of AC. Shield = 1 stone. Six ordinary items = 1 stone.

## The cart

**Small cart, one mule** (Korvane's, papered, bought for 1sp):

| | Normal load | Maximum load |
|---|---|---|
| Capacity | **50 stone** | **75 stone** |
| Expedition speed | **12 miles/day** | **6 miles/day** |

Teodor has **Driving**, which *doubles the road movement bonus* — on a real road he can push
a cart to something like 24 miles in a day. On a cart-track to a hamlet nobody has visited in
eight years, that bonus doesn't exist.

## Speed of the column

Everything moves at the pace of the slowest thing in it.

| | Expedition speed |
|---|---|
| Teodor (encumbrance 6½ st) | 18 miles/day |
| **Goblins (Move 60′)** | **12 miles/day** |
| **Cart at normal load** | **12 miles/day** |

So a column of goblins and a loaded cart makes **12 miles a day**, and the two constraints
happen to match exactly.

## Eating

**Judge's ruling** (the book gives no goblin ration figure): a goblin eats about **half** what
a man eats. The band is 10 adults and 2 young.

> **Grett's band eats roughly 6 stone of grain a week.**

Hunger, by the book: less than full rations for a day → **hungry** (−1 to attacks,
proficiency throws, saves). Two days with nothing, or five days averaging half, →
**underfed** (no forced march, no natural healing). Then **starving**, which is 1 CON a day
until you're dead.

## They can hunt

This is the part worth remembering. **Hunting** is a dedicated activity: a 14+ throw per
hunter per day, and a success brings in 1 stone of game — enough for six man-sized creatures,
which is twelve goblins.

Ten adults hunting bring in something like three stone a day against a need of less than one.
**The band is not helpless and never was.** They robbed the road because they were camped
three miles from a town, where the game is thin and where a goblin seen hunting is a goblin
killed. Twelve miles out, with nobody watching, they feed themselves.

*Foraging* is worse — an 18+ throw, at −2 in borderlands country — and not worth planning
around.

So grain is a **bridge**, not a permanent obligation: enough to carry them from the Cistern
to somewhere they're allowed to live.

## Grain, keeping of

- **Whole grain, dry, off the ground, rats kept off it: keeps a year.**
- **Ground meal: weeks.** Grind what you're about to eat.
- Therefore: buy whole grain, and buy a **hand quern** to grind it with. A dry building
  matters more than the sack does.
