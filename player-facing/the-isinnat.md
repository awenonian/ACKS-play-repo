# The Isinnat — the rite Teodor is building

*Player-facing. This is Teodor's own working, not a scholar's. It may be wrong in places.
He is reconstructing a feast-rite out of a debt-collector's curse tablet, which is a bit like
reconstructing a wedding out of an eviction notice.*

## The source

A lead *defixio*, palm-sized, folded and nailed, recovered from the votive deposit at the
Cistern spring-head. Scratched with a stylus, not cut — a private man at his own table.
Roughly two thousand years old. Translated:

> **Below. To the Ones Who Are Under. To the mouths that do not close.**
>
> I, Ashur-nadin, of the house of Belesh, son of Ashur-kin, of Zerath-Kal:
> I set down the man Zakuti, son of Nabu-etel.
> He holds nine shekels of my silver, and eleven months are gone.
> **I give him into my hand, and out of my hand into yours,** as a man gives a slave into a hand.
> He is yours to the marrow. Let none loose him — not his god, not his house, not his father.
> Only I may loose him. **And I do not.**
>
> It is set down. It is sealed. **It is eaten.**

**Five joints:** who is addressed · who speaks · who is named · **what passes into whose
hand** · the sealing.

## Vocabulary that matters

| Word | Means | Note |
|---|---|---|
| ***maddat*** | tribute, what is taken | Teodor used it correctly for Vess Kestrin's boots |
| ***kurummat*** | the issue, the ration, what comes out of a store | what is on the cart: salt pork, cheese, dried apple |
| ***shiru*** | **flesh** — the meat of a thing that was alive | the feast word. Votive inscriptions use it. A barrel cannot supply it |
| ***isinnat*** | the festival-issue | the thing Teodor promised, before he knew what it was |
| ***abu*** | father | **unseverable in both directions.** A lord may release. A father cannot stop being one |
| ***sarru*** | **lord** — specifically the protective kind, whose justification is that he keeps his people | duty is inside the word, both ways, and it *can* be ended. Also the first half of **SARRU-KALYX**, cut on a milestone three miles south |

**"It is eaten"** is boilerplate — the sealing on deeds, betrothals, land sales, receipts for
oil. It appears on a great many documents in which nothing at all was eaten. An odd verb for
a language to have settled on for a land sale.

## Teodor's version (draft)

> I, Teodor, of the house of Vask, son of Caldric:
> I set down this feast. It is of ~~meat~~ **[kurummat or shiru — undecided]**, and fruit, and cheese.
> I give it out of my hand, into yours, as a ~~father gives to a son's hand~~ **[abu or sarru — undecided]**.
> It is yours to eat. With it we are bound by duty. Let us not forsake our duty. I do not.
>
> It is set down. It is sealed. It is eaten.

**No invocation.** Teodor struck it and did not replace it. Nothing above the two parties and
nothing under them — the obligation stands on itself. For a man whose living is other men's
paper, that may be the only honest answer available.

## The tokens

Twelve wrist-scraps of **faded woad blue**, cut from a half-bolt bought off Sarn for 18 silver.
Teodor's own invention; no source suggests it. Chosen over grey because twelve goblins coming
up a track wearing mourning cloth would read, to anyone who saw them, as a raided funeral.
