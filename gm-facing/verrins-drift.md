# GM-Facing: Verrin's Drift & The Mill Men

**Status: authoritative. This is what is true.**

Twelve miles north-west of Tellys Ford, on the **Verrin brook**, a small tributary that
joins the Krysivor four miles downstream. The "drift" is the gravel ford where the north
track crosses the brook.

## The Place

Nine houses of stone footing and turf wall, a walled sheepfold, and a **mill** — the only
two-storey building, stone, roof sound. Abandoned about eight years.

**Why it emptied, exactly:** eleven years ago a spring flood shifted the brook's channel
about two hundred yards west. The mill went dry. Without the mill the freeholders were
carting grain twelve miles to the ford to have it ground, and paying for the privilege, and
they left one and two families at a time over three years. The Verrins went last.

**Why it is off every rent-roll:** the freehold reverted to the Verrin family. The last
heir, **Cassa Verrin**, took a soldier's wage and died at Hasp two years ago. Nobody has
filed a claim. The tax clerk at the ford struck it from his list after three years of no
payment, and the provincial rolls are four years stale because there is functionally no
province any more.

**What it is worth:** nine sound wall-lines, a defensible sheepfold, good pasture gone to
thorn — and the brook can be put back. Roughly a fortnight of ditching for twelve pairs of
hands. A working mill twelve miles from a ford town is real money, every year, forever.
*This is Teodor's first domain and he does not know it yet.*

Korvane named the Drift because it is empty, out of the way, and off the books. **She is
not lying and she does not know about the mill men.** The robberies happen six miles further
north, at the bend where the Krysea road turns — a road that does not cross her ford and
therefore does not cost her a copper. She has heard the north road is "bad this spring" and
has filed that under everything else being bad.

## The Mill Men — seven survivors of Wier

Eighteen months ago the fort at **Wier**, on the eastern line, fell. Eleven men walked out.

They went south to **Kalyx Gate**, because that is what everyone does.

### The Nine Days (Renn's story — the truth, and he tells it straight if he tells it)

Prefect Ostren took them in. Fed them, gave them a barrack row, had the physicker to their
wounded, and offered them enlistment in the Third Cohort. He was courteous and did not press.

On the eighth day **Nonnus** — Renn's optio, gut-wounded at the river crossing — died.

Renn asked for a pyre. He was told, kindly and with regret, that fuel was short (**true** —
the Waste side of Kalyx has no timber) and that the burning-ground lay outside the wall and
outside the wall was not safe (**true**). He was offered a grave inside the compound. He took
it. He buried Nonnus in the ground.

Then he lay awake a night and a day, and on the tenth morning he took the men who would
follow him and walked out the north gate.

**Three of the eleven stayed and enlisted in the Third: Ruvo, Bett, and the boy Tullo.**
There has been no word from any of them in eighteen months. Renn assumes shame, on one side
or the other. *The truth is that men of the Third cannot leave.*

### What Renn will actually say

"They don't burn their dead." Then, in the same breath, before anyone can respond:
*"And that's their business, and there's a war on, and I'll not say a word against the
prefect. He's a better officer than ever I was."* **He means the last part.**

He cannot explain why it frightened him. He knows exactly how it sounds — a superstitious
ranker who would not take good shelter over a rite — and it has cost him six months in a
dead mill robbing carters while eleven miles of the safest road in the province sat there.
**His own men know the arithmetic too.**

## Solution Space (rank-ordered for this character)

1. **Broker them to Korvane.** Seven real legionaries against her eleven green levies is not
   close. She would pay, and she can give them legal cover — a toll-holder with an imperial
   grant has at least colourable authority to retain men. This makes Teodor indispensable to
   two parties at once and hands Korvane the company she is already building. **Best play,
   and it accelerates the domain game deliberately.**
2. **Buy them out.** Light infantry wage is 6gp/month; a month for seven is 42gp. Cheap —
   but they need somewhere to *go*, and that is the actual problem, and Teodor knows it
   because he just solved it once.
3. **Split them.** Piso will leave. Marc and Otho follow whoever is talking. Cato takes coin.
   Hurn never leaves Renn. This gets the Drift but breaks seven men into pieces.
4. **Coexist.** Twelve goblins and seven legionaries in one hamlet. Absurd, very funny,
   genuinely a domain seed. Allow it. Make it hard. Neither side sleeps for a week.
5. **Fight.** Achievable — a night assault with twelve slingers who see in the dark against
   men in a stone building is a real plan. It will cost lives, it will cost Renn's knowledge,
   and Kalyx Gate's second confirmation goes in the ground with him.

## Things To Not Do

- Do not let Renn deliver Kalyx Gate as a warning. It is a thing he is embarrassed about.
- Do not make him a villain. He robs carters and hates it.
- Do not have anyone connect "no pyres" to anything. Nobody has a theory. Nobody ever has.
