# GM-Facing: Standing Rulings

*Judge's calls made in play. These are now true and must stay true.*

## Bestiary gap
This rules bundle has **no monster entries** — ACKS II keeps them in a separate volume.
Creature stats are derived from `armies/troop-characteristics.md` (same rulebook) plus
explicit Judge's calls, and the player is told which is which.

## Lightless vision
**Goblins have lightless vision at 60'.** Ruling, not citation — the bestiary is missing.
60' is the baseline figure the rules give for the ability (`campaigns/magic-experimentation.md`
grants "Lightless Vision 60'", upgrading to 90' if already possessed). Applies to all
goblins in this campaign. Orcs likewise when they appear.

## Goblin language comprehension
Teodor knows **Ancient Zaharan** (parent) and **Orc** (daughter). Goblin is Orc's sibling,
both descended from Ancient Zaharan per `setting/entries-1.md`.
**Ruling: Teodor can make himself understood in Goblin, haltingly.** Functional for trade,
threats, and simple questions; not for nuance. He sounds archaic and strange — a man
ordering dinner out of scripture. **+1 on reaction rolls** with goblins for the novelty of
being spoken to at all.

## Venturer hit dice
The class entry states **1d6** in three places. The optional "Blessed by the Gods" sidebar
(p.18) lists venturers at 4, which is the d4 figure — stale text. **Class entry wins.**
Flagged to the player.

## Helmets
Light helmets are **included in armor cost**; going without reduces AC by 1. Corrected an
earlier error where Hoyt was described as helmetless at AC 3 — AC 3 was right, the flavor
was wrong. He now also has a **heavy helm** (Enclosing: −1 surprise, −4 Listening,
**+2 on Mortal Wounds table**).

## Reaction rolls in play
Rolled openly, 2d6 + CHA + Diplomacy + situational Judge's bonus, stated before rolling.
Influence attempts cost escalating time (1 round, 1 turn, 6 turns, 8 hours, 5 work-days).
Shift interpretation: 12+ = 2 steps friendly, 9–11 = 1 step, 6–8 = no change, 5− = 1 step
hostile. Unmodified 2 never better than Unfriendly; unmodified 12 never worse than Indifferent.
