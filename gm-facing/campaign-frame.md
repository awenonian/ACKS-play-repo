# GM-Facing: Campaign Frame — "The Hungry Gate"

**Status: authoritative. Nothing here is uncertain. This is what is true.**

System: ACKS II Imperial Imprint. Setting: the Auran Empire, **the Borderlands** province.
Year: 4 years after the Tarkaun took the legions west against the Skysos. 1 year after the
rumor arrived that they were encircled and destroyed.

---

## The One-Paragraph Truth

The border fort of **Kalyx Gate** is the only fort on the northern Borderland line that
has not fallen. It is praised across the province. Refugees flee toward it. The beastmen
of the Waste will not go within ten miles of it. All of this is true, and all of it is
because its prefect fed it to something.

---

## The Layers, Innermost First

### Layer 3 — What is actually under the fort

**Sarru-Kalyx**, a sorcerer-priest of the Kingdom of Zahar, dead for roughly 2,100 years
and not remotely finished. He is Chthonic faithful in the strict sense (see setting/gods):
he does not believe in the cycle of rebirth. He believes the soul is *consumed as fuel*
at death. He therefore preserved his body and his singular identity intact in a
cenotaph-vault beneath the hill, to survive until **the Awakening**.

He is not waiting passively. He is *accumulating*. Souls that die within his reach and
are **not burned** (Empyrean rite: burn the body, Calefa ushers the soul, Türas weighs it)
do not depart. They bank. He is close — decades, not centuries — and the western war has
been extraordinarily good for him.

He cannot leave the vault. He can reach roughly to the walls of the fort above him. He
speaks to exactly one living man.

### Layer 2 — The bargain

**Prefect Naevio Ostren**, commanding the remnant of the Third Cohort, XII Legion.
Two years ago his pay stopped, his relief never came, and the fort to his east fell with
all hands. He went down into the cistern-shaft his engineers had opened while repairing a
foundation, and he came back up having agreed to terms.

The terms: *the fort does not fall, and his soldiers do not die.* Men of the Third Cohort
who take a mortal wound get up again. They are not undead in any way a layman would spot —
they breathe, eat, talk, remember their mothers. They are pledged. What they have lost is
the ability to ever leave, and their death is now a formality Sarru-Kalyx can call in.

The price: **the dead are not burned at Kalyx Gate.** Not soldiers, not travelers, not
the beastmen killed at the wall, not the refugees who die of their wounds inside it. There
are no pyres. There has not been a pyre at Kalyx Gate in two years.

Naevio is not a cultist and does not think of himself as damned. He thinks of himself as
the last officer on this border still doing his job, paying a price no one else was willing
to pay. He is largely correct about the first part. He is a competent, tired, courteous man
and the hardest thing about him is that he is *likeable*.

### Layer 1 — What the province sees

- Kalyx Gate holds. Everything else on the line is falling.
- The roads within a day of Kalyx Gate are the safest in the Borderlands.
- Beastmen raid everywhere *except* the approaches to Kalyx Gate.
- Prefect Ostren is spoken of as the one honest man left in imperial service out here.
- People who take shelter at Kalyx Gate sometimes come home. Sometimes they don't. Nobody
  has done the arithmetic, because who counts refugees.

### The name

The Aurans named the fort for the hill. The locals called the hill *Kalyx*. "Kalyx" is
2,100 years of Danean mouths wearing down **Sarru-Kalyx**. The fort is named after the
thing buried under it and not one living person knows that. A character who learns
Ancient Zaharan, or who asks the right scholar, can find this out. It is the single
cleanest piece of evidence in the campaign and it is sitting in plain sight on every
imperial map.

## Arc Shape (mutable in detail, fixed in direction)

- **Levels 1–3:** local Borderlands trouble around **Tellys Ford**. Kalyx Gate is only ever
  a rumor and a direction on the map. Establish that it is *good news*.
- **Levels 4–6:** the party has reason to go to Kalyx Gate, and Naevio is a gracious host.
  The horror is that nothing is wrong.
- **Levels 7+:** the arithmetic gets done. Naevio must be chosen for or against. The vault.
- **Domain game:** the Borderlands power vacuum is real and the campaign is built to hand
  the player a piece of it. Lady Korvane (below) is the natural rival-or-partner for that.

---

## Standing NPCs (definite)

**Lady Dessa Korvane** — holds the toll rights on the Tellys Ford crossing by an imperial
grant her great-grandfather bought. Mid-forties, widowed, sharp, unsentimental. She is
quietly raising a private company of foot because she has correctly concluded no one is
coming to save the ford. She is *not* a villain. She is what the setting says is happening:
a local warlord filling a vacuum. She thinks well of Prefect Ostren and says so.

**Father Anselm Vurro** — a priest of Calefa (Lady of Fortune, Mother of Mourning) at Tellys
Ford, keeper of the burning-ground. Elderly. Fussy about rite. He is the character most
likely to notice the pyre problem, because it is literally his job, and he is the campaign's
early alarm bell. He does not know anything yet.

**Prefect Naevio Ostren** — see Layer 2. Late thirties. Krysean-born, which is a small
shame he doesn't discuss (Krysea resisted the Empire for a century). Speaks softly, keeps
his boots clean, remembers names.
