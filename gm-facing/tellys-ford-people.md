# GM-Facing: Tellys Ford — People & State

*Definite. No uncertainty here.*

## Standing

**Lady Dessa Korvane** — toll rights, 11 green men in her yard. Reaction with Teodor:
**Friendly** as of the deal. Has agreed to buy Teodor's 120gp note off Sarn (~95gp,
discount she disclosed voluntarily) and **strike it entirely** when the south cut opens.
Waived her landowner's half of any treasure, publicly. Lent the small cart and grey mule.
Two conditions: **Hoyt goes**, and **Hoyt comes back**.
Bought her title's dignity with her great-grandfather's money and is sensitive about it —
responds to arithmetic, not deference. Told Teodor the truth about her own men when he
named it, and has not forgiven herself for enjoying being told.

**Vitte Sarn** — factor. Holds the note until Korvane's letter reaches him this afternoon.
Not a lender by trade; will sell at a discount to be rid of the risk. Keeps the cancelled
Kalyx Gate order pinned to his ledger board and has never once thought about it.

## The taproom (Merrow's) — **UNFRIENDLY to Teodor**

Cause: he walked out of the toll-house and put a gold sun on the bar in a town of 400.
Reads as Korvane's money and Korvane's man. This is a *standing* condition, not a one-scene
mood — it persists until something changes it. Clearing the road would change it. Coming
back with Vess Kestrin's body would change it differently.

**Kestrin** — shepherd, ~50. Son **Vess**, 14, went out after strays on the 11th, down the
ditch line toward the fort. Kestrin walked to the arch twice and called. **The dog came
back on the third day, unmarked.** He asked Teodor for the boy's boots.
*(Vess is dead — cold and a broken leg, in the silt at the north end of the head tank. The
goblins took his boots and left him. It is not a murder and it should not be softened.)*

**Antar** — carter, nine years of Teodor's business. First to turn back at the arch.
Gave it up grudgingly: stones out of the rubble, **in daylight**, and "jabbering — fast,
high voices, weren't Auran and weren't Krysean."

**Merrow** — publican. Not friendly, just professional. Wanted the temperature out of his
room and paid for it with a name.

## New

**Doret Kell** — ropewalk, blue door. As a girl, ~60 years ago, she was on the crews that
stripped the old fort for building stone; half the bridge-head is out of it. **She knows
the underground works** because they tried to get at them and gave up. She is the map.
She is also genuinely unpleasant — not a puzzle to be solved with charm, a woman with a
low opinion of the whole enterprise. *Decide what she wants before Teodor knocks.*

**Nikkos Varr** — 15, tanner's youngest. Offered to come for free. **Refused, gently.**
Gave up his information anyway, which is the thing to remember about him.
Has been down the gate stair with the Ferrin boys two summers back: the head chamber is
dry dirt; **past it the works are flooded and it comes up the steps in spring.** Tam Ferrin
put a leg in past the knee and found no bottom.
He is the same age as the boy in the silt, near enough, and the town is already looking at
him that way. Succession candidate if Teodor dies.

**Hoyt** — carter nine years, now one of Korvane's eleven. Took a sling stone in the
shoulder at the arch ten days ago and ran. 0th level, AC 3 (leather + shield), spear,
4 hp, Save F0, **Morale −1**. Being told where he's going right now.

## Live facts Teodor has learned
- Below the head chamber: **flooded, and high — late spring.**
- Attackers use slings, act in daylight, are small (waist-height per Hoyt), and chatter
  in a language that is neither Auran nor Krysean. *(Goblin. Teodor has not made the
  connection out loud yet.)*
