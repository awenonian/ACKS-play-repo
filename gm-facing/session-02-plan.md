# GM-Facing: Session 2 — "The Naming"

**PC:** Teodor Vask, Venturer 1, **2 hp**, 231 XP. **Henchman:** Hoyt, 0th, 110 XP.
**Opens:** morning of the 16th, Tellys Ford. Late spring.

---

## The Design Problem

Session 1 was solved entirely with words and nobody rolled an attack throw. That was correct
and it should not become a guarantee. Session 2 must put a **thing that will actually kill
him** in the road — seven legionaries in a stone mill — and let him find out about it in time
to not walk into it. The horror-of-the-fort plot advances by *ordinary bitterness*, not by
discovery.

**The clock:** the band has six days of food. After that they rob the road again. Teodor
knows this if he thinks about it; Grett will say it plainly if asked.

**The chain:** the promise (isinnat) → the move (twelve goblins past a town of 400) →
the destination (which is occupied).

---

## ACT ONE — Tellys Ford, the 16th

Loose scenes. Let the player pick order. Nothing here is gated.

### The problem he has to solve first: what did he promise?
He said *festival-issue*. He does not know what it entails. Ways to find out:
- **Ask Grett** (requires going back to the Cistern first — inverts the act order, fine).
- **Reason it out from Zaharan** — he reads the language. A player who thinks about what a
  slave empire's feast-day ration would consist of gets there. Reward that.
- **Ask Vurro.** A priest of Calefa knows what a rite is *for* even across a religion. He
  will be scandalised by the question if it is asked plainly, and useful if it is asked
  crookedly — as a merchant's question about old Zaharan custom, say.
See `the-cistern-band.md` for what it actually is.

### Provisioning
- **Korvane** funds grain through Sarn's warehouse, described as fodder. She has already
  agreed. The meat and drink for the rite are Teodor's own problem and his own money.
- **Vitte Sarn** — the note is struck. He is relieved and slightly diminished by it. He will
  sell fodder without blinking, but he is a factor, and a man who just cleared a road buying
  grain by the *cartload* is an arithmetic problem. **Sarn does not accuse. Sarn remembers.**
- Charcoal and a chisel for the milestone: any smith. Trivial.

### Doret Kell — owed an answer
The mark has not moved; the floor rose three feet. She wanted this. Give it to her plainly
and she gives back, because she is not charmed, she is *paid*:
- **The upstream crossing at Ammet's Stones**, two miles above the bridge — gravel bar,
  passable at a walk when the river is not in spate, which it currently nearly is. This is
  how you move twelve goblins past a town without using Korvane's bridge.
- **Verrin's Drift.** She is seventy and has lived here all her life. She knows why the mill
  went dry, she knows the Verrin heir died at Hasp, and she will say — with satisfaction,
  because she enjoys other people's failures — that *"nobody's filed on it, and nobody will,
  because there's no one left to file with."* **This is the domain hook, delivered as spite.**
- Her brother's bones are still past the second run. She has not asked. She will not ask.
  If Teodor offers, that is a debt she cannot easily carry and she will be unpleasant about it.

### Hoyt
He has a heavy helm, 110 XP, and a standing request: **"ask me."** He will not lie to Korvane
if asked direct. He has now watched Teodor lie to a room and tell Korvane the truth privately,
and he has not decided what that makes him. If Teodor asks him anything real, he answers.

### Kestrin
Grateful. Burned his son on the 15th with four ells of Teodor's grey. He is going up to the
high pasture above the Verrin brook in a few days for the summer grazing — *he mentions this
as small talk*. It is the route. Do not underline it.

## ACT TWO — The Cistern, the 16th or 17th

### The isinnat
See `the-cistern-band.md`. Three parts: meat killed that morning by his own hand, drink,
and the naming. If Teodor arrives without meat, Grett's face does something and she says
nothing, and that is the scene.

### Naff's refusal
The centrepiece. Eleven answer the name. Naff does not. Full treatment in
`the-cistern-band.md` — including what he gives up if Teodor is patient with him in private,
which is the best information in the Borderlands and will sound like nothing.

### The 520gp
Still on a saddle blanket in the head tank. **520 coins = 0.52 stone** — it is not heavy, it
is *conspicuous*. Carrying it to Tellys Ford earns the XP and invites the question of where a
man got a sack of century-old Krysean garrison pay. Leaving it invites a different question.

### The milestone
Rubbing with charcoal and grey cloth, then deface, was his stated plan. Let him do it. The
rubbing is a physical object he now owns: **SARRU-KALYX — XI** in Ancient Zaharan on grey
mourning wool. If he ever shows it to a scholar, that is the campaign.

---

## ACT THREE — The road north-west, night of the 17th

Twelve miles. Goblins move 60′; a night march with a cart and a lame goblin and a mother
carrying an infant is **two nights**, or one very hard one. Options for the crossing:
Ammet's Stones (Doret), or ask Korvane to pull her toll-man off the bridge for one night
(she would, quietly, and it costs him a favour and entangles her further).

**Encounters, chosen not rolled** — pick at most one:
- Kestrin's remaining boy on the high pasture with a dog, at dawn. The dog goes berserk.
- A carter on the Krysea road who has been robbed and is walking home. **This is the first
  news of the mill men and it arrives as a complaint about the state of the roads.**
- Nothing. A hard cold night and twelve frightened goblins on an open road is enough.

## ACT FOUR — Verrin's Drift

**Smoke from the mill chimney.** Everything in `verrins-drift.md`.

The scene shape: Teodor is standing on a hill above a hamlet he was promised was empty, with
twelve goblins behind him, looking at chimney smoke. Whatever he does next, he should be
allowed to **scout before committing** — and if he pushes the column down the track without
looking, Silus is in the loft with a crossbow and Teodor has 2 hp.

## XP Budget

- **520gp** from the Cistern, XP on reaching a friendly settlement. That is the session's
  bulk and it is *already earned* — the tension is logistical, not heroic.
- Renn's seven, if defeated by any means including brokered away: Renn (F1) 10 XP + six
  0th-level (5 each) = **40 XP**. Trivial. **The point is not the XP.**
- Korvane will pay for veterans if brokered — that is reward treasure, XP when received.
- A clean session puts Teodor near **800–900 XP**. Level 2 at 1,500. Correct pace.

## Things To Not Do

- Do not let Renn's "they don't burn their dead" be received as a revelation. It is a
  crank's grievance and everyone including Renn treats it that way.
- Do not have Korvane know about the mill men. She doesn't. She is not testing him.
- Do not resolve Naff this session unless the player does the work.
- No end-of-turn thread reports. `open-threads.md` only, surfaced in a lull.
