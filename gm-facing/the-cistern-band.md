# GM-Facing: Grett's Band

**Status: authoritative.**

Twelve goblins in the head tank of the Cistern, three miles south of Tellys Ford. Refugees
out of the Waste, six weeks east of the border. Reaction **Friendly**. Fed once, by Teodor,
as *kurummat* — an issue, in the Zaharan sense, from a holder to his people.

## The *isinnat* — the festival-issue

Teodor said *festival-issue* and it landed heavier than he meant. In the Zaharan register the
band inherited (via Orc, via two thousand years of hold-custom), the **isinnat** is the feast
ration a holder issues on a god's day. It has three parts and they all matter:

1. **Meat that was alive that morning**, killed by the holder's own hand in front of them.
   Grain is *kurummat*. Grain is what you feed people you own. Meat is different: it proves
   he has something to give and gives it.
2. **Drink.** Beer, or wine, or anything that is not water. Any amount. It is the *category*
   that matters.
3. **The naming.** The holder speaks his own name and his house. The bond-folk answer with
   it. This is the whole rite; the rest is dinner.

**If the naming happens and is answered, they are his.** Not slaves — the Zaharan word does
not distinguish, but the *practice* the goblins inherited from orc holds is closer to
clientage. They are his people. They expect to be fed, placed, and spent, in that order.

### Costs (Tellys Ford, Class VI market; Class V for Teodor via Mercantile Network)
Sheep 2gp · Pig 3gp · Goat 3gp. Two sheep and a pig feeds twelve to bursting. A small cask
of ale is a few sp. **The whole rite costs under 10gp.** The cost is not money.

### Mechanical result
The band ceases to be an encounter and becomes a **retinue** — treat as goblin slingers
(3gp/month wage if he ever pays them; he has not offered and they have not asked).

- **Rite performed properly, Naff's refusal handled:** Loyalty **+2** (base 0, CHA +1, rite +1).
- **Rite performed, Naff's refusal left standing:** Loyalty **+1**, and the band carries a
  visible crack that Naff will widen.
- **Fed but no naming:** no retinue. They stay Friendly and they stay *someone else's problem*,
  which is a legitimate outcome and Teodor should be allowed to choose it.
- **Coerced (Naff killed, band cowed):** loyalty **−4**. Forced servitude. Do not soften this.

## NAFF

Forty years old, which for a goblin is old. Grey around the muzzle. One of the four scouts
who came back to find the band pledged to a man. **The "short argument" was his.**

### What he actually knows — and it is worth more than the paychest

**Twenty-two years ago**, on the Waste side, Naff's band was taken up by an orc named
**Ghaz Mur**. Ghaz Mur fed them, named them, and spent them in a single season against a
rival hold. Nine of eleven died. Naff walked out of it and has not trusted a full belly since.
*He has no words for this that a man would credit, so he does not try.*

**Eight years ago** Naff ranged the Waste-side approaches to the hill the beastmen call the
hungry hill — the hill the Aurans call Kalyx. He has seen it with his own eyes:

- Within about a day's walk of the hill, **the old orc holds stand open and empty.** Not
  burned. Not looted. Doors open, pots on the hearths, nothing taken — because nobody will
  go in and take it.
- **No game. No birds.** A ring of country maybe ten miles across with nothing living in it.
- And the thing he saw himself, from a scrub line at dusk, after a clan hit the wall and was
  broken on it: **the soldiers came out and carried the beastman dead back in through the
  gate.** Not out to a fire. In.

To Naff this is simply what the hungry hill is and needs no explanation. To an Auran it means
"they take trophies," which is ugly but not strange. **It is the truest thing anyone in this
campaign will say for a long while and it will sound like nothing.**

### How Naff behaves

**At the naming, Naff does not answer.** Publicly, in front of eleven. He does not shout, he
does not draw. He simply stands with his mouth shut, and everyone sees it.

Teodor's options and what they cost:

- **Kill him / have him killed.** The band becomes his at −4 loyalty. Forever.
- **Cast him out.** Clean, cheap, respected — and Naff's knowledge walks into the Waste.
  Tul may go with him.
- **Ignore it.** The rite is diminished. Loyalty +1. Naff stays and widens the crack.
- **Demand an answer in front of the band.** He cannot give one there. He will stare at the
  floor and the band will read it as insolence and it gets worse.
- **Ask him privately, and be patient.** This is the win, and it should cost real time —
  an influence attempt at the 6-turn or overnight tier, not one round. He gives up Ghaz Mur
  first. The hungry hill only comes out if Teodor keeps listening after he has what he wanted.

## What the band expects, having been fed once

- To be **placed**. They understand they are going somewhere. They do not know where.
- To be **spent**, eventually. They are not naive about this. Grett is not naive about it.
- Provisions: Teodor left roughly 13gp of food. **Six days of it left as of the 16th.**
  After that they will take from the road again, because the road is what there is, and
  Grett will not be able to stop them and will say so if asked.
