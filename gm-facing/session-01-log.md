# Session 1 Log — "Grey Cloth"

**Played:** character creation through the burning of Vess Kestrin. Two in-game days.
**Attack throws rolled: zero.** By anyone, in either direction. The session was decided by
reaction rolls, one sentry's eyes, and six searching throws in the silt.

## What happened

Teodor Vask, a Borderlands-born venturer holding thirty bolts of unsellable grey mourning
cloth and a 120gp note, took a job from Lady Dessa Korvane to clear the south cut.
He haggled her from 60gp to the full 120 by making the toll-revenue argument and by telling
her, to her face, that her eleven levies could not take that ruin. She sent Hoyt with him —
the man who ran — and made bringing him back a condition.

He spent his only gold sun buying drinks for a taproom that wouldn't drink them (snake eyes;
unmodified 2 caps at Unfriendly regardless of modifiers) and got the room's cold shoulder and
Kestrin's grief for it. Merrow gave him a name to get him out: Doret Kell, who as a girl
carted the fort away stone by stone and whose father drowned two men trying to reach the
paychest. She gave him the map: spring-fed, unlowerable, eighteen inches of clearance in the
settling runs, and *anything on your back that catches, catches.*

He refused Nikkos Varr, who gave up his information anyway.

Approached at dusk, unseen. Read the airflow at the stair mouth with his fingers over the
lip and learned the hill exhales at night. Was spotted by the sentry on a roll of 1 —
and could not be surprised, because the matrix +1 and Alertness +1 put him past the ceiling.

Then he shouted a Zaharan legal formula down a hole at twelve starving refugees, and the
whole session turned on it. His archaic register made him sound like a magistrate of the
empire that *bred* them; *apart from the spears* read as **above** the spears. He never
corrected it. He fed them, took Vess's boots as *maddat*, and issued the food as *kurummat* —
because a slave empire's language has no word for a gift between equals.

They told him about the box of "yellow round things" they had been starving on top of for
five weeks, because a coin is only a promise that someone will trade with you, and no one
in the Empire would sell them bread. He offered assessment rather than seizure. Grett
noticed, out loud, that he could have said *maddat* and taken the lot.

**620 gold suns**, carried up in handfuls by small swimmers through drowned stone that killed
two men in mail. Plus ~85gp from the silt: bronze windlass gear, garrison coin, four stone
of iron stock, and a votive deposit at the spring-head with Zaharan platinum and electrum
in it and a two-thousand-year-old curse tablet from a man who was owed money.

**The milestone was read.** SARRU-KALYX — XI. He clocked that *sarru* means lord and that
you don't carve a man onto a road-stone unless the road ends at him. Filed as a curiosity.

Back in town he lied once, publicly and well — *too many for two men, we scared them off,
the road's clear but maybe not the ruin, there's work still to do* — which bought Kestrin's
gratitude and shifted the taproom Unfriendly → Neutral, and which cost him something he
noticed. Then told Korvane the whole truth in private, unprompted.

She proposed blooding her eleven on the twelve. Said it out loud, as arithmetic, because
she says her arithmetic out loud. He talked her off it on the numbers — twelve defending
against eleven green levies is a *cheap* fight, not a safe one, and men who butcher the
starving come home coarse, not brave. She rolled a 14. She agreed and went further:
**Verrin's Drift**, twelve miles north-west, off every rent-roll; she funds the grain through
Sarn's warehouse as fodder; Teodor does all the talking; she comes to him if she ever needs
what they are. And she gave him Hoyt outright.

Vurro bought four ells of grey for the boy — the first sale off that lot — and remarked,
as a professional irritation and nothing more, that a fort with no need of mourning cloth
is a fort that isn't burning its dead.

## Where the campaign stands

The player has the campaign key in his hands twice over and does not know it: the cancelled
order with *no further need* on it, and a milestone naming a lord eleven miles south.
Vurro has stated the mechanism of the horror out loud without knowing he did.

Nothing has been forced. Every clue arrived as somebody's ordinary business.
